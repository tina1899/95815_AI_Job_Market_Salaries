**Dataset:** AI Jobs Market 2025–2026 Salaries

**Publisher:** Syed Ali Taqi (`alitaqishah` on Kaggle)

**Source:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/alitaqishah/ai-jobs-market-2025-2026-salaries

**Pinned version:** 1, released March 6, 2026.

**License reported by Kaggle:** CC0 Public Domain. The publisher's original metadata snapshot is retained in `raw/kaggle_view_metadata.json`.


The exact downloaded ZIP and extracted source CSV are included without editing. The download time, public download URL, version and checksums are in `raw/source_manifest.json`.

The publisher describes the dataset as compiled from salary reports and job-market research, listing Ravio, Robert Half, Motion Recruitment, LinkedIn and Glassdoor. Those are publisher claims, not independently verified row-level sources. The file contains no original posting URL, employer-name field or full job description. This repository does not establish that the records are raw scraped postings, and course acceptability under the real-world-data requirement needs confirmation.
